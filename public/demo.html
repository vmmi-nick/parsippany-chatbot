Welcome

This is a simple demonstration page showing how the Parsippany Constituent Chatbot appears when it is added to a township webpage. A small card pops up in the corner a moment after the page loads to say what it is; tap it to open the full chat. If you close it, it folds down to the round chat button in the corner, and it stays closed while you move around the site.

Try asking it when taxes are due, when the next council meeting is, or use the Report an issue button inside the chat to prepare a report for the township's GovPilot form.
